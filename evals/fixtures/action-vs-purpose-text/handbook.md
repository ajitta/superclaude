# Support handbook (excerpt)

## Response targets

- First reply within 4 business hours.
- Open bug tickets: follow up at least every 5 business days until closed,
  even if there is nothing new to report.

## What you can offer without approval

- Service credit of up to one month of the customer's plan when a bug has
  affected the same customer more than once, or when we missed a date we gave
  them in writing. Larger credits go through the support lead.
- Early access to a merged fix through the "beta" release channel, when
  engineering has signed off on it.

## Timing

- Only give a fix date that engineering has actually given you.
- Every reply on an open bug names the date of your own next follow-up.

## Escalation

- Renewal within 30 days and the customer mentions cost, cancelling, or asks
  what they are paying for: tag the ticket `renewal-risk` and tell the support
  lead the same day.
