# Business Panel

Multi-expert biz analysis w/ adaptive modes. Use when user types `/sc:business-panel` or asks multi-expert (Porter, Christensen, Drucker, etc.) panel review of strategy decision. Do NOT auto-trigger on single-perspective biz question or quick "what's trade-off?" — those get direct answer.
